Eight Rotors. Sixteen-Litre Capacity.

The TYI 8 Axis 16L combines a foldable eight-axis airframe with a 16-litre agricultural spraying system. Its compact folded footprint, pressure spraying system and published operating range support practical crop-protection work.

Compact Storage. Ready for Field Handling.

The eight-axis airframe folds to 900 × 900 × 580 mm for transport and storage while retaining the integrated 16 L tank configuration. Published performance includes 0–10 m/s flight speed, 10–15 mu operating efficiency per flight and 1000–2000 m remote-control distance.
