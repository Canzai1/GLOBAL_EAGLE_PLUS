Discuss This 10 L Work Plan

How many batteries should I plan for repeated 10 L spraying flights?

Ask for a proposal that separates batteries required for the offered aircraft configuration from any additional batteries for your intended work schedule. Request the matching battery and charging-equipment identifiers and the assumptions behind the proposed quantities. This page does not specify a battery-turnaround time or an extra-battery count.

Can the published figures establish my daily spraying coverage?

Not on their own. The page lists 10–20 minutes of flight time, 10–15 mu per flight and a 3–5 m spraying width. Ask which payload, application rate and field conditions these figures represent, and how a proposed workday accounts for refilling, battery turnaround and travel between plots. They are not a daily-output guarantee.
