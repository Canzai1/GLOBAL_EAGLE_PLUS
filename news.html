Agricultural UAVBuying Guide

Agricultural Spraying Drone Buying Guide for Crop Protection Projects

An agricultural spraying drone should be selected around the crop type, field size, spraying width, tank capacity, droplet performance, and local service plan. For distributors, customization and spare parts availability are also important because customers often need different nozzles, pumps, flight settings, and payload options.

  • Confirm spraying scenario: open field, orchard, rice field, greenhouse, or mixed terrain.
  • Compare tank capacity, spraying system, payload structure, and flight stability.
  • Prepare OEM/ODM needs such as logo, color, documentation, and distributor packaging.

VTOL UAVMission Planning

How VTOL Drones Support Long-Endurance Mapping and Inspection

VTOL drones combine vertical takeoff with fixed-wing cruise efficiency. This makes them useful when a project requires long range but has limited launch space. Buyers should evaluate mission distance, payload weight, cruise endurance, control range, and data workflow before choosing a VTOL platform.

  • Best fit: mapping corridors, infrastructure routes, wide-area patrols, and long-distance inspection.
  • Key buyer questions: endurance, payload compatibility, route planning, and maintenance support.
  • Customization may include camera mount, payload wiring, branding, and operation documentation.

Industrial UAVApplications

Industrial Inspection Drone Applications for Power and Facility Patrols

Industrial inspection drones help teams reduce manual risk, collect visual data faster, and reach areas that are difficult to access. Common applications include power line patrols, facility inspection, pipeline monitoring, park security, emergency response, and public safety support.

  • Choose the platform based on operating environment, payload, camera needs, and mission duration.
  • Inspection buyers should ask about payload mounting, real-time monitoring, and field deployment.
  • Factory support matters for training, spare parts, troubleshooting, and repeat project delivery.

Surveying UAVPayload Guide

Mapping Drone Payload and RTK Considerations

For surveying and mapping projects, buyers should confirm the required output before selecting a drone. Orthophotos, 3D models, construction measurement, land planning, and environmental monitoring may require different payloads, route settings, and data processing workflows.

  • Confirm image resolution, RTK positioning requirements, and route planning needs.
  • Match the aircraft type to the terrain, area size, payload weight, and data deliverables.
  • Ask whether documentation and training can be adapted for distributors or project teams.

CustomizationChecklist

What OEM/ODM Buyers Should Prepare Before Drone Customization

A clear OEM/ODM brief helps reduce communication time and improves project accuracy. Buyers should define application field, quantity, branding, payload requirements, target market, local regulations, packaging needs, and after-sales expectations before production planning begins.

  • Prepare logo, color, label, packaging, and documentation requirements.
  • Share payload, endurance, field environment, and operation workflow expectations.
  • Confirm sample plan, production schedule, quality inspection, and spare parts needs.

How to Evaluate a Drone Production Line Service Partner

For customers planning a local UAV assembly or production capability, the partner should provide more than product supply. Important support includes production flow planning, equipment matching, testing and calibration process guidance, staff training, and long-term technical communication.

  • Evaluate whether the partner understands product assembly, QC, and delivery workflow.
  • Ask about training, documentation, testing process, and production equipment matching.
  • Confirm support for future product updates, spare parts, and project expansion.
