Integrated project scope

Agricultural platform definition

Spraying or spreading mission, tank or payload capacity, airframe, propulsion, control system and annual output are converted into a defined project basis.

Assembly process engineering

Frame, arms, landing gear, propulsion, tank, pumps, hoses, nozzles or spreader, wiring and avionics are arranged into a controlled build sequence.

Application-system integration

Liquid-path sealing, pump and flow setup, nozzle configuration, spreading mechanism and related calibration points are defined for the selected platform.

Equipment & fixtures

Assembly tools, torque controls, fixtures, electrical test equipment, calibration tools and facility interfaces are specified within the approved scope.

Testing & acceptance

Incoming inspection, leak and flow checks, electrical safety, calibration, ground testing and agreed flight-test or FAT/SAT criteria are documented.

Documentation & training

The project package can include BOM, drawings, work instructions, inspection records, maintenance information, configuration control and role-based training.

Project delivery framework

  1. Platform & application review: Confirm spraying or spreading use, target capacity, aircraft configuration, annual output, localization objective and destination market.
  2. Feasibility & scope: Define responsibilities, selected local processes, deliverables, exclusions, acceptance basis, schedule assumptions and commercial boundaries.
  3. Line & process design: Prepare workstation flow, agricultural drone assembly sequence, equipment plan, quality gates and the approved documentation package.
  4. Pilot build & FAT: Build and verify pilot agricultural drone units, check the application system and close identified process issues before factory acceptance.
  5. Installation & SAT: Support installation, commissioning and site acceptance against the agreed line configuration, calibration method and test plan.
  6. Training & production ramp: Train production, quality, test and service teams, then support initial output, issue resolution and controlled handover.

Frequently asked questions

Which agricultural drone platforms can the line cover?

The scope can be developed around an approved four-, six- or eight-axis spraying or spreading platform. The exact model, capacity, components and test requirements must be confirmed before quotation.

Can one line support more than one tank capacity?

Potentially, when the selected agricultural drone models share compatible processes, tooling and quality controls. The common and model-specific stations are defined during line design.

Are spraying and spreading systems included?

They can be included as approved integration and testing workstreams. Pumps, hoses, nozzles, flow calibration, tanks or spreading mechanisms are specified for the selected configuration.

Are equipment and tooling included?

They can be included in the agreed project scope. The final equipment list, fixtures, utilities and procurement responsibilities are confirmed after the production target and facility review.

Do you provide production and maintenance training?

Training can cover agricultural drone assembly, inspection, calibration, ground and flight testing, application-system maintenance, troubleshooting and production management. Duration and location are confirmed in the contract scope.
