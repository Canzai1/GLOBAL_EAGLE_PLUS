Frequently Asked Questions

Products and configuration

What types of drones are listed on this website?

The current catalog includes agricultural drones, cargo and delivery drones, FPV drones and brushless motors. Product pages show the available model-specific information.

Which agricultural drone tank sizes are listed?

The listed agricultural platforms include published 5 L to 30 L tank or load capacities across four-, six- and eight-axis models. Check the individual product page for the selected model.

Can the 30 L agricultural platform be used for spraying and seeding?

The TYI 4 Axis 30L platform is presented in spraying and seeding configurations. Please confirm the required module and final configuration before ordering.

What delivery-drone payloads are available to discuss?

The listed cargo-drone range includes published payload classes from 5 kg to 50 kg. Final configuration and operating performance depend on the selected model and conditions.

Quotes and inquiries

What information should I send to request a quote?

Please share the product or model of interest, intended application, required tank size or payload, purchasing quantity, destination country and your contact details.

Can I discuss an OEM or ODM configuration?

Yes. Send the intended application and requested configuration. We can review the requirements with the relevant technical team; feasibility, scope and terms are confirmed case by case.

Are published flight times guaranteed for every operation?

No. Published flight times and ranges describe listed configurations. Actual performance can vary with payload, battery, weather and operating conditions.
